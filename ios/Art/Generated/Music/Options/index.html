MediEval Music Options

Four original chiptune loops with a stronger medieval flavor. The recommended Court Minstrels track is bundled as the app default.

Recommended

1. Court Minstrels 6/8

Most medieval-feeling: lute-like plucks, modal melody, light percussion, and a longer 32-second arrangement for judging cards without getting too intense.

2. Castle Fanfare

More heroic and arcade-like. Good for the title screen or Guild Hall, but maybe a bit too bright for long eval sessions.

3. Dungeon Scribe

Darker and calmer. Useful if the app should feel more focused and less bouncy while reviewing lots of cards.

4. Tavern Quest Jig

Fun and lively. Very medieval-gamey, but it may become the most repetitive during real work.
